---
title: "Set Invisible"
---

## Description

<big>Causes one or more players to become invisible to either all other players or just enemies.</big>

## Snippet

```
Set Invisible(Event Player, ALL);
```

## Properties

**Returns**: _<span style="color: #c678dd">Void</span>_
**Parameters**: ==_Player_==, ==_Invisible To_==

> ==**Player**==
> _Type: **[Player](/constants/player/)** | **Array** (**Player**), Default: **[Event Player](/values/event-player/)**_
> The player or players who will become invisible.

> ==**Invisible To**==
> _Type: **Invis**, Default: **ALL**_
> Specifies for whom the player or players will be invisible.
