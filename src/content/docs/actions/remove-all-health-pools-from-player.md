---
title: "Remove All Health Pools From Player"
---

## Description

Removes all health pools that were added to a Player or Players via the Add Health Pool action.

## Snippet

```
Remove All Health Pools From Player(Event Player);
```

## Properties

**Returns**: _<span style="color: #c678dd">Void</span>_
**Parameters**: ==_Player_==

> ==**Player**==
> _Type: **[Player](/constants/player/)** | **Array** (**Player**), Default: **[Event Player](/values/event-player/)**_
> The Player or Players whose added Health Pools will be removed.
