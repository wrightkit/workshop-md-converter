---
title: "Enable Inspector Recording"
---

## Description

<big>Causes the workshop inspector to resume recording new entries (in case it had been disabled earlier). Enabling recording at specific times may make it easier to debug problematic areas in your logic.</big>

## Snippet

```
Enable Inspector Recording;
```
