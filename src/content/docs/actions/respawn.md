---
title: "Respawn"
---

## Description

<big>Respawns one or more players at an appropriate spawn location with full health, even if they were already alive.</big>

## Snippet

```
Respawn(Event Player);
```

## Properties

**Returns**: _<span style="color: #c678dd">Void</span>_
**Parameters**: ==_Player_==

> ==**Player**==
> _Type: **[Player](/constants/player/)** | **Array** (**Player**), Default: **[Event Player](/values/event-player/)**_
> The player or players to respawn.
