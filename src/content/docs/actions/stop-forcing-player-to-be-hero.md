## Description

<big>Stops forcing one or more players to be a specific hero. This will not respawn the player or players, but it will restore their hero availability the next time they go to select a hero.</big>

## Snippet

```
Stop Forcing Player To Be Hero(Event Player);
```

## Properties

**Returns**: _<span style="color: #c678dd">Void</span>_
**Parameters**: ==_Player_==

> ==**Player**==
> _Type: **[Player](/constants/player/)** | **Array** (**Player**), Default: **[Event Player](/values/event-player/)**_
> The player or players who will no longer be forced to be a specific hero.
