## Description

<big>Sets the damage dealt of one or more players to a percentage of their raw damage dealt.</big>

## Snippet

```
Set Damage Dealt(Event Player, 0);
```

## Properties

**Returns**: _<span style="color: #c678dd">Void</span>_
**Parameters**: ==_Player_==, ==_Damage Dealt Percent_==

> ==**Player**==
> _Type: **[Player](/constants/player/)** | **Array** (**Player**), Default: **[Event Player](/values/event-player/)**_
> The player or players whose damage dealt will be set.

> ==**Damage Dealt Percent**==
> _Type: **Float**, Default: **[Number](/values/number/)**_
> The percentage of raw damage dealt to which the player or players will set their damage dealt.
