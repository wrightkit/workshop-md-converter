---
title: "Impossible Things in Workshop"
---

This article was created as a tutorial for Workshop beginners.
There are some things that cannot be created or tracked in Workshop.

## **Abilities**
* Abilities cannot be made invisible.
 * Technically, you can make barriers invisible using ``Set Invisible``.
* Objects owned by a player cannot be duplicated. (For Example, 2 or more Venom Mines or Steel Traps)
* Player objects (BOB, turrets) do not trigger relevant player events.
* Effects cannot be directly added to abilities.
* Abilities cannot be scaled.
 * Technically, You can scale barriers using ``Start Scaling Player`` or ``Start Scaling Barriers``.
* Attack speed of weapons cannot be directly modified.
