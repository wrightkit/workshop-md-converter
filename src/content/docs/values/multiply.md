## Description

<big>The product of two numbers or vectors. A vector multiplied by a number will yield a scaled vector. The product of two vectors is equal to the product of each of their components; that is, `Vector(A, B, C) * Vector(D, E, F) == Vector(A * D, B * E, C * F).`</big>

## Snippet

```
Multiply(0, 0);
```

## Properties

**Returns**: _<span style="color: #c678dd">Float</span>_ | _<span style="color: #c678dd">Vector</span>_
**Parameters**: ==_Value_==, ==_Value_==

> ==**Value**==
> _Type: **Float** | **Vector**, Default: **[Number](/values/number/)**_
> The left-hand operand. May be any value that results in a number or a vector.

> ==**Value**==
> _Type: **Float** | **Vector**, Default: **[Number](/values/number/)**_
> The right-hand operand. May be any value that results in a number or a vector.
