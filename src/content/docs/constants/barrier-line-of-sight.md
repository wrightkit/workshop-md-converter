---
title: "Barrier Line of Sight"
---

### Description
Used in [Is In Line of Sight](/values/is-in-line-of-sight/). Defines how barriers affect line of sight. When considering whether a barrier belongs to an enemy, the allegiance of the player provided to start pos (if any) is used.

### Options
- **Enemy Barriers Block LOS**
  - Line of sight is blocked by barriers created by the enemy team.
- **All Barriers Block LOS**
  - Line of sight is blocked by all barriers.
- **Barriers Do Not Block LOS**
  - Line of sight is not blocked by any barriers.
