---
title: "Progress Bar HUD Reevaluation"
---

## Description

Used in [Create Progress Bar HUD Text](/actions/create-progress-bar-hud-text/). Specifies which of this action's inputs will be continuously reevaluated. The text will keep asking for and using new values from reevaluated inputs.

## Options

- **Color**
- **None**
- **Values**
- **Values and Color**
- **Visible To**
- **Visible To and Color**
- **Visible To and Values**
- **Visible To, Values and Color**
