---
title: "Progress Bar In-World Text Reevaluation"
---

## Description

Used in [Create Progress Bar In-World Text](/actions/create-progress-bar-inworld-text/). Specifies which of this action's inputs will be continuously reevaluated. The text will keep asking for and using inputs from reevaluated inputs.

## Options

- **Color**
- **None**
- **Position**
- **Position and Color**
- **Position and Values**
- **Position, Values and Color**
- **Values**
- **Values And Color**
- **Visible To**
- **Visible To and Color**
- **Visible To and Position**
- **Visible To and Values**
- **Visible To, Position, and Color**
- **Visible To, Position, and Values**
- **Visible To, Position, Values, and Color**
- **Visible To, Values, and Color**
