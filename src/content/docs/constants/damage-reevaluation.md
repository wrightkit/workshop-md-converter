---
title: "Damage Reevaluation"
---

## Description

Used in [Start Damage Modification](/actions/start-damage-modification/). Specifies which of the action's inputs will be continuously reevaluated. The action will keep asking for and using new values from reevaluated inputs.

## Options
- **None**
- **Receivers and Damagers**
- **Receivers Damagers and Damage Percent**
