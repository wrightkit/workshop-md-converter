---
title: "Icon Reevaluation"
---

## Description

Used in [Create Icon](/actions/create-icon/).  Specifies which of this action's inputs will be constantly reevaluated. The icon will keep asking for and using new values from reevaluated inputs.

## Options
- **Color**
- **None**
- **Position**
- **Position and Color**
- **Visible To**
- **Visible To and Color**
- **Visible To and Position**
- **Visible To, Position and Color**
