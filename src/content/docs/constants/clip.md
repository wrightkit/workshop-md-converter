---
title: "Clip"
---

## Description

Used in [Create In-World Text](/actions/create-inworld-text/). Specifies whether the text can be seen through walls or is instead clipped.

## Options
- **Clip Against Surfaces**
  - The text may be partially or completely obscured by walls, floors, ceilings, players, or other solid objects.
- **Do Not Clip**
  - The text will always be fully visible, even if it is behind a wall or solid object.
