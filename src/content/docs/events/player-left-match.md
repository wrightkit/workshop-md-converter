---
title: "Player Left Match"
---

This event type triggers for a player when they leave the match and the `Conditions` evaluate to `True`.

Note that the player's variables and any other information about them are no longer available at this point.

#### Arguments

*  Team - The team(s) checked by the event.
*  Player - The player(s) checked by the event based on slot number or hero.

#### Contextual Value

*  Event Player - The player that triggered the event.
